# 🚀 Hecho en Osa 2026 · Pro builders

You already code. You build wherever you like, in your own GitHub account, public or private. You don't follow the Zero to Hero weeks and you don't need a repo from us.

## What you do

| When | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Once, at the start | Send your idea | **[🚀 Pro project form](https://github.com/hechoenosa/pro2026/issues/new?template=pro.yml)** |
| Every Sunday, 8 pm | 3 questions: what you did, what's next, blockers | **[📈 Pro weekly update](https://github.com/hechoenosa/pro2026/issues/new?template=pro-update.yml)** |
| Once, during the program | Meet your mentor | Your Mentor Room on WhatsApp |
| By Thu 10 Dec | Your PRD, TRD and a demo link | Comments on your 🚀 Pro project issue |
| Sat 12 Dec | Show it | Demo Day |

Want a starting structure? Copy [PRD.md](https://github.com/hechoenosa/project-template/blob/main/PRD.md) and [TRD.md](https://github.com/hechoenosa/project-template/blob/main/TRD.md) into your own repo.

Questions? Ask your mentor in your Mentor Room.

¡Hecho en Osa! 🌴
